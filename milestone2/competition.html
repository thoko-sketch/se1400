My main inspiration is Baba is Hint a guide to Baba is You that, rather than being a direct walkthrough, offers hints to guide players through levels so they can figure out the puzzles for themselves rather than just watching someone else solve them.

While I want BIH style hints is the main spotlight here, I also want to include some more comprehensive walkthroughs for these levelpacks, as these would be unavailable anywhere else. I'd also like to include a dictionary for any modded words. Overall, I just want to create a comprehensive guide to anyone getting into BIY levelpacks.
