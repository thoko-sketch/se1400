Website Summary

What content will the site contain?

Guides and walkthroughs for various Baba is You fan-created levelpacks.

Where will you get all of the content for your site (ie. images & text)?

Images and assets will be found at the Baba is You discord server and wiki. Screenshots and videos will be sourced from my own playthrough on Steam.

Why should users care about the focus of the site?

Baba is You is a fantastic game that has spawned many creative fanmade levelpacks over the years. Unfortunately, many of these levelpacks can be very intimidating to newcomers who don't feel "Smart Enough" to solve the levels. By having a dedicated website to help guide people through these levels, the BIY modding community can expand its reach.

Why should a site be built for this?

Baba is You's main game already has a similar inspired guide, Baba is Hint, which is very popular among fans. It only seems natural that some popular levels get a guide as well.

What would you like users to do at your site?

Use the various guide on offers to allow them to complete Baba is You levelpacks without resorting to to the use of a walkthrough and/or encourage them to start said levelpacks with the help of a support system.

What is the goal of the site?

To make the various Baba is You modpacks as acessible as the main game.
